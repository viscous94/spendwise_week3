How to use this tracker

Use this form to record and keep track of your expenses. Enter the details of each expense and click the Add Expense button.

  1. Enter the name of the expense.
  2. Enter the amount you spent.
  3. Select the expense category.
  4. Select the date of the expense.
  5. Click Add Expense.

Your recorded expenses will appear in the Your Expenses table.
